### A bit of background

My route into development wasn't a straight one.

After secondary school (Informaticabeheer) I spent four years at Devine in Kortrijk, studying visual design, typography and UI/UX. It didn't feel like the right fit at the time, so I left without the diploma and went to work: first as a PC technician at Computer Checkpoint, later in logistics at Becton Dickinson in Temse.

Somewhere along the way I realised I still wanted to make websites. So I went back to Howest for the graduaat Web Development & Design and finished it Cum Laude in 2025.

After that I spent a year at Expertum, first as an intern and then as a Junior SAP / Front-End Developer. I built front-end components in TypeScript, Web Components and SAP UI5, connected them to REST and OData services, and dug into existing RAP and ABAP code when something broke.

Looking back, the detours were useful. The design years shaped how I look at interfaces, and the technician job taught me to stay calm and debug step by step.

### How I work

I treat Claude (Anthropic's AI) as a colleague. I use it to think through a problem, to review my code, to explain a library I haven't touched before, and to take care of the boring bits so I can focus on the parts that matter. What goes into a commit is still my call: I read everything, test it and make sure I understand why it works.

The same goes for design. I care about accessibility, performance and the person who ends up using the thing. A site that looks great but doesn't work for its users isn't finished.
