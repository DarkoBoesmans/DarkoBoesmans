## Hi, I'm Darko

Front-end developer and designer from Lebbeke, Belgium. I like being involved in a feature from the first sketch to the last commit: drawing it out in Figma, then building it myself.

I'm currently looking for my next job as a junior developer (front-end or full stack) in Flanders.

### A bit of background

My route into development wasn't a straight one.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| before 2018 | Secondary school, Informaticabeheer |
| 2018 to 2022 | Devine (Howest, Kortrijk): visual design, typography and UI/UX. Didn't feel like the right fit yet, so I left without the diploma |
| 2021 to 2025 | Logistics at Becton Dickinson in Temse, as a student and interim worker |
| 2022 to 2023 | PC technician at Computer Checkpoint in Lokeren |
| 2023 to 2025 | Back to Howest for the graduaat Web Development & Design, finished Cum Laude |
| 2025 to 2026 | Expertum: intern, then Junior SAP / Front-End Developer |
| now | Looking for my next job and learning C# and .NET |

At Expertum I built front-end components in TypeScript, Web Components and SAP UI5, connected them to REST and OData services, and dug into existing RAP and ABAP code when something broke.

Looking back, the detours were useful. The design years shaped how I look at interfaces, and the technician job taught me to stay calm and debug step by step.

### How I work

I treat Claude (Anthropic's AI) as a colleague. I use it to think through a problem, to review my code, to explain a library I haven't touched before, and to take care of the boring bits so I can focus on the parts that matter. What goes into a commit is still my call: I read everything, test it and make sure I understand why it works.

The same goes for design. I care about accessibility, performance and the person who ends up using the thing. A site that looks great but doesn't work for its users isn't finished.

### Projects worth a look

**[BumperPool](https://github.com/DarkoBoesmans/BumperPool)**
Belgian tap billiards (golfbiljart) in the browser, in TypeScript. You walk into a smoky low-poly café with a PS2 look and play by the official GZV rules. There's a story mode where you work your way through 18 regulars in different bars, ending up in a TV studio. I wanted the rules to be exactly right, so every article of the rulebook is in there.

**[Portfolio](https://github.com/DarkoBoesmans/DarkoBoesmans.github.io)** ([live](https://darkoboesmans.github.io))
Built with Astro, TypeScript and SCSS. Dutch and English, light and dark mode, accessible, no tracking, no cookies and no third-party requests. The whole site weighs about 600 kB.

**[Waste app for IVAGO](https://github.com/DarkoBoesmans/stage-expertum-wasteapp)**
My internship project at Expertum: a proof of concept where citizens of Ghent can report illegal dumping with a photo. SAP UI5 and TypeScript on top of SAP CAP, with SAP AI Core to categorise the reports.

**[Waste container detection](https://github.com/DarkoBoesmans/stage-expertum-detectron2)**
Also from the internship: training a Detectron2 model in Python to spot waste containers in images, from setup to predictions.

**[Garderobe](https://github.com/DarkoBoesmans/garderobe)**
A lookbook and wardrobe app in TypeScript and SCSS (ITCSS and BEM), with a contrast check that fails the build if colours don't meet WCAG AA.

**[Chiro Popov](https://github.com/DarkoBoesmans/ChiroPopov)**
The websites for my Chiro, the Bosfeesten festival and Project P. I did the design, the copy and the front-end as a volunteer.
