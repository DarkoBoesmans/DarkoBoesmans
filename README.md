## 👋 About me

I'm a **Design Engineer and Front-End Developer** from Belgium who likes to own both sides of a feature: sketching it in Figma, then building it in clean, accessible code.

- 🎓 Graduated **Cum Laude** in **Web Development & Design** at Howest, on top of a visual design foundation from **Devine** (Digital Design & Development).
- 💼 Spent a year at **Expertum** building production front-end components in **TypeScript, Web Components and SAP UI5**, wired to **REST and OData** services in an enterprise SAP environment.
- 🔐 Run my own **Linux home server** (Docker, Portainer, Tailscale) to keep control over my own data, and a **local AI lab** with Ollama that I use in my daily dev workflow.
- 🌱 **Next up:** growing from front-end into **full stack**, with **C# / .NET** on the back-end side.
- 🏕️ Off-screen: ex-leader at **Chiro Popov** (2017 to 2026), runner, hiker, and saving up for the next backpacking trip.

> **"Design should be functional, not just pretty."**
> A website shouldn't only impress. It has to work for the people who use it.
